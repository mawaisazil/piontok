About PionTok

PionTok is a free, fast, and privacy-respecting video downloader that helps you save content from TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube without watermarks. Our mission is to give creators and viewers an honest, lightweight tool that just works — no sign-ups, no ads in your face, no data harvesting.

Our Mission

We believe the web should be open and user-controlled. PionTok removes the friction between you and the videos you love — letting you archive, remix, and enjoy content on your own terms, on any device, in any language.

Technology

PionTok is built as a single-page application using semantic HTML, modern CSS custom properties, and vanilla JavaScript ES modules. It ships as a Progressive Web App with offline support, a custom service worker, and a manifest for installable use on mobile and desktop. No frameworks, no trackers, no external assets beyond the Google Font.

Team

We're a tiny distributed team of frontend engineers and designers who care about user experience. We maintain PionTok in our spare time and listen to every feedback email.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What data we collect

None. PionTok does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. URLs you paste are sent directly to the upstream video API and never written to our servers. Your download history is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and is never synced anywhere.

Cookies

PionTok uses only essential storage: a theme preference, a language preference, and (optionally) an anonymous session token when you log in. No third-party tracking cookies are set.

Local storage

The following keys may be written to your browser: piontok_theme, piontok_lang, piontok_history, piontok_user, piontok_session. You can clear all of them by clicking "Clear History" or by wiping site data in your browser settings.

Third-party APIs

Video metadata is fetched from the public tikwm.com API (or a fallback mock). We do not control what data those endpoints log; please review their respective privacy policies if you have concerns.

Tools

A growing collection of free, browser-based utilities built with the same speed-first, privacy-respecting philosophy as the main downloader. All tools run entirely in your browser — no server-side processing, no tracking, no signup.

Video Downloader

The flagship tool. Paste a TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube link to download the video without a watermark in HD or SD quality. Supports audio-only extraction (MP3) and TikTok image/slideshow posts. Powered by the tikwm.com API with a 10,000-request-per-cycle quota.

Audio Extractor

Pull the original sound or music track out of any video as a clean MP3 file. Useful for saving trending TikTok sounds for your own creations, building playlists of background music, or extracting interview audio from long-form videos. The audio is extracted at the source quality — no transcoding, no quality loss.

Thumbnail Grabber

Download high-resolution cover images and video thumbnails. Works for any TikTok or YouTube video. Useful for content creators who want to study what makes a thumbnail clickable, or for archiving the cover art of a favorite clip. Returns the original full-resolution image, not a compressed preview.

Hashtag Generator

Enter a topic or keyword and get a curated list of trending hashtags ranked by reach and competition. The generator pulls from current TikTok and Instagram trending data and groups tags by niche (e.g. #fyp, #foryou, #creator, #smallcreator) so you can mix broad-reach and niche tags for maximum discoverability.

Video Length Analyzer

Paste a video URL and the analyzer breaks down its pacing — average shot length, longest take, fastest cut, and total runtime. Helpful for editors studying what makes short-form content feel snappy versus dragging.

Batch Downloader

Coming soon. Paste up to 50 video URLs at once and PionTok will fetch them in sequence, with a progress bar and a ZIP archive of all downloaded files at the end. Available exclusively in the Pro tier.

Built different

Every tool on this page ships as part of the same single-file PWA. That means they work offline (after first load), install to your home screen, and inherit the dark/light theme and language you've already chosen. No separate downloads, no separate accounts, no separate permissions.

How to download TikTok videos without a watermark

The definitive guide. We walk through the three ways TikTok stamps a watermark on shared videos, why each one exists, and how PionTok's pipeline strips every trace of them — from the bouncing TikTok logo to the username overlay — without re-encoding the video. We also cover the legal side: when it's fair use to download a clip and when it isn't.

Why HD downloads are slower (and how to speed them up)

HD videos are 3–5× larger than SD, which means longer download times. We explain why the tikwm.com API returns both URLs, how the browser's native download manager handles range requests, and why the direct-URL approach (used by PionTok) is faster than the blob-buffer approach used by most other downloaders.

The tech behind PionTok's 200KB single-file PWA

A deep dive into how PionTok fits an entire video downloader — multi-platform URL detection, i18n in four languages, dark/light theming, PWA with offline support, an in-browser mock auth system, download history, a service worker, and ~80 unit tests — into a single 200KB HTML file with no build step. Spoiler: vanilla JS, CSS custom properties, and an inline SVG sprite sheet.

Understanding TikTok image posts (slideshows)

TikTok's slideshow format — a carousel of still images synced to music — is increasingly popular but poorly supported by most downloaders. We explain how the tikwm.com API exposes the `images` array, how PionTok renders each image as its own downloadable quality option, and why the resulting files are higher resolution than the video version of the same post.

i18n in vanilla JS: four languages, zero dependencies

How we built a translation system in 50 lines of vanilla JS that supports English, Spanish, French, and Arabic — including RTL layout flipping — with no framework, no compiler, no polyfill. The `data-i18n` attribute pattern, the `translations` object structure, and the runtime swap function are all explained.

Privacy by design: what we don't store

A frank walkthrough of PionTok's data flow. URLs you paste go directly to tikwm.com — never to our servers. Download history lives in your browser's localStorage and never syncs anywhere. Theme and language preferences are local. We don't set tracking cookies. We explain why this matters and how to verify each claim using your browser's devtools.

Building a service worker in 60 lines

The PionTok service worker is small but complete: cache-first for core assets, network-only for the API, background cleanup of old caches, and a `skipWaiting` escape hatch. We annotate every line and explain why each decision was made.

The case against React for small projects

An opinionated take. PionTok ships in 200KB. A comparable React app would be 1.5MB+ before any application code. We discuss when framework overhead is worth it (large teams, complex state, frequent refactoring) and when it isn't (single-purpose tools, performance-critical UIs, projects that need to load on slow connections).
